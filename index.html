📖 How to Play

Everything you need to rule the watermelon multiverse.

01

Tap to harvest

Tap the big watermelon to harvest melons. Tap power grows as you buy farms and upgrades.

02

Buy farms

Farms produce melons every second — even while you're away. Double them with upgrades.

03

Prestige (⭐)

Reach ~1M melons and Ascend to earn Seeds. Seeds permanently boost ALL production. Collect 10+ for powerful permanent upgrades.

04

Play Suika

The classic watermelon-stacking game is built in! Every merge earns your empire melons. Chain combos, beat your best.

05

Quests & drops

Do quests for crystals & XP. Clicks, buys and golden melons drop crates with rare collectibles. Fill your collection!

06

Level up

Earn XP from everything. Every level = +2% production, +1% tap power and free crystals. Show it off on the board.

07

Skins & currency

Spend 💎 crystals on skins and boosts. Your live balance is shown on the leaderboard — buying updates it in real time.

08

Events

Random global events hit the whole multiverse at once — Meteor Showers, Crystal Rains, Fruit Rushes and more. Catch them live!

❓ FAQ

How do I save my progress?

Automatically — it's saved in your browser's local storage every few seconds. No account needed. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

Where does the leaderboard live?

Every player shares one free keyless cloud store (MantleDB), so the board is truly global and updates in real time. Give it a few seconds to sync.

What are Seeds and Prestige?

When you Ascend you convert your run's melons into Seeds. Each seed gives +1% permanent production (upgradeable to +10%). It's the core end-game loop.

How do I get crystals 💎?

Daily rewards, achievements, quests, golden melons, crates, roulette and Suika milestones. Spend them on skins, boosts and permanent shop upgrades.

How does the Suika mini game help my idle empire?

Every fruit merge adds melons to your empire, big merges drop rare collectibles, and making a watermelon grants a legendary drop + crystals.

What are the global events?

Random timed events (Meteor Shower, Crystal Rain, Gold Rush, Fruit Rush, Turbo) affect the whole multiverse at once. Tap the banner when it appears.

I found a bug!

Tell Dave-VR! This game is hand-built with love (and melons) and every report makes it better.
