📖 How to Play

Everything you need to rule the watermelon multiverse.

🚀 Quick Tutorial

01

Tap to harvest

Tap the big watermelon to harvest melons. Tap power grows as you buy farms and upgrades. Hold to auto-tap!

02

Buy farms

Farms produce melons every second — even while you're away. Buy ×1, ×10, ×25 or ×100 and double them with upgrades.

03

Prestige (⭐)

Reach millions of melons and Ascend to earn Seeds. Seeds permanently boost ALL production. Use seeds for powerful permanent upgrades.

04

Play Suika

The classic stacking game is built in! Press Start, drop matching fruits to merge them. Every merge earns your empire melons.

05

Whack-a-Melon

Whack the melons that pop up before they vanish. Gold melons are worth ×5! Score becomes melons and drops.

06

Roulette

Spin for crystals! Win up to 100 💎 or a ×2 Suika boost. Costs 10 💎 per spin (discounts reduce it).

07

Quests & drops

Do story + daily quests for crystals & XP. Clicks, buys, golden melons and bosses drop crates with collectibles. Fill your collection!

08

Level up

Earn XP from everything you do. Every level = more production, more tap power and free crystals.

09

Bosses 👹

Every 45–80 minutes a Boss Watermelon attacks! Smash it fast for big crystal + crate rewards. Beat your own record!

10

Global events

Random events hit the whole multiverse at once — Meteor Showers, Crystal Rains, Fruit Rushes and more. Tap the banner!

11

Golden melon

A glowing golden melon appears randomly. Click it fast — it can be a Lucky Jackpot, Frenzy, Tap Storm or Time Warp!

12

Skins & currency

Spend 💎 crystals on skins and permanent shop boosts. Your live balance updates on the leaderboard in real time.

❓ FAQ

How do I save my progress?

Automatically — it's saved in your browser's local storage every few seconds. No account needed. Clear your browser data and it's gone.

Where does the leaderboard live?

Every player shares one free keyless cloud store (MantleDB), so the board is truly global and updates in real time. Your current melon score updates when you buy or spend.

What are Seeds and Prestige?

When you Ascend you convert your run's melons into Seeds. Each seed permanently boosts production. It's the core end-game loop.

How do I get crystals 💎?

Daily rewards, achievements, quests, golden melons, crates, roulette, bosses and Suika milestones. Spend them on skins, boosts and permanent shop upgrades.

How does the Suika mini game help my idle empire?

Every fruit merge adds melons to your empire, big merges drop rare collectibles, and making a watermelon grants a legendary drop + crystals.

What are the bosses?

A Boss Watermelon attacks randomly every 45–80 minutes. Tap it to deal damage before the timer runs out. Defeat it for crystals, crates and XP.

What are the global events?

Random timed events (Meteor Shower, Crystal Rain, Gold Rush, Fruit Rush, Turbo) affect the whole multiverse at once. Tap the banner when it appears.

I found a bug!

Tell Dave-VR! This game is hand-built with love (and melons) and every report makes it better.
